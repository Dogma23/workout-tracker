Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date of publication]

Lift Tracker helps you log workouts. This page explains what information the app keeps, where it's kept and what you can do about it. It's written in plain English on purpose.

Without an account

If you don't turn on cloud backup, everything stays on your device. We never receive it. Deleting the app (on iPhone, the home-screen icon) deletes that data, so use Settings → Back up now to keep a copy.

With cloud backup turned on

When you create an account, we store the following so it can be backed up and restored on your other devices:

  • Account: your email address and a securely hashed password (we never see or store your actual password).
  • Training data: your workout history (exercises, sets, weights, reps, how each set felt), your training plan and your settings.
  • Profile: name, goal, experience, equipment, avatar and health information you choose to enter — the body areas you want to protect and any conditions note.

Health information is sensitive. We ask for your consent before storing it in the cloud, and you can leave those fields blank.

Why we keep it

Only to run the app for you: to back up your data, restore it on a new device and keep your devices in sync. We don't sell your data, show ads or share it with advertisers.

Where it's kept and who can see it

Data is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase, in their Sydney, Australia region. It's encrypted in transit and at rest. Access rules mean each account can only read its own data. The only other people with technical access are the app's administrator, and only to keep the service running.

How long we keep it

For as long as you keep your account. Deleting your account deletes your cloud data.

Your choices

  • Export a copy any time: Settings → Back up now.
  • Correct your details in the app; changes sync automatically.
  • Delete your account and all cloud data: You → Cloud backup → Delete account. Data on your device is not affected.
  • Sign out to stop syncing; your data stays on the device.

Contact

Questions or requests: [contact email]. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
